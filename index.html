Volleyball: The Sport of the Net

Rules, skills, and player positions explained

What is Volleyball?

Volleyball is a team sport played by two teams separated by a net. The goal is to make the ball touch the ground on the opponent's side of the court, while preventing it from touching the ground on your own side. Each team may touch the ball up to three times before sending it back over the net.

Basic Skills

  • Serve: the action of putting the ball in play by hitting it over the net to start a rally.
  • Reception: the first team contact, used to control the opponent's serve and direct the ball to the setter.
  • Setting: passing the ball with precision so a teammate can attack it effectively.
  • Attack: hitting the ball forcefully into the opponent's court to score a point.
  • Block: jumping at the net to stop or redirect the opponent's attack.
  • Defense: digging and moving to keep difficult balls off the floor and in play.

Player Positions

Volleyball teams rely on five key positions, each with a specific role on the court.

Libero

The Libero is a defensive specialist who wears a different colored jersey and cannot attack the ball above net height. Their main job is to receive serves and dig hard-driven attacks, keeping the ball alive for the team.

Setter

The Setter is the playmaker of the team. After the ball is received, the Setter decides where to place it, setting up teammates with accurate passes so they can attack effectively.

Outside Hitter

The Outside Hitter attacks from the left side of the court and is usually the team's primary scorer. They also take on serve-receive and defensive duties, making them one of the most complete players.

Opposite

The Opposite plays on the right side of the court, facing the Setter. They are a key attacking option, especially against strong blocks, and often help block the opposing team's Outside Hitters.

Middle Blocker

The Middle Blocker attacks quickly through the center of the court and is responsible for reading the opponent's offense to form blocks against attacks from any position.
